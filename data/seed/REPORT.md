## 2. Method

Pipeline (all scripts in `tools/seed/`, Python 3 stdlib only; run in this order):

1. `fetch_egymonuments.py`: egymonuments.gov.eg EN/AR sitemaps plus the portal's own map-pin feed (`/Umbraco/Api/MapsWebAPI/GetAllMapPins`) to enumerate every museum, archaeological-site and monument page (24 museums, 34 archaeological sites, 125 monuments). Only page URLs and titles are kept.
2. `fetch_mota_tickets.py`: MoTA's official ticket-price list (PDF, last updated 5 Nov 2024, https://mota.gov.eg/media/5a2ja2iu/ticket-english-5-11-2024-1.pdf), text-extracted to list which sites/museums are ticketed. It was used only to decide which sites to include and to set the `mota` flags; no prices or hours were copied into the seed. The egymonuments portal's archaeological-sites section has only 34 entries, so this list was the main source for ticketed sites.
3. `fetch_wikipedia_lists.py`: AR `قائمة متاحف مصر` and EN `List of museums in Egypt`. Only row names and section headings are kept, resolved to QIDs. These lists were used to cross-check the museum set.
4. `fetch_wikidata.py`: museum enumeration and details for every museum, curated site and duplicate QID (labels, aliases, P31, P625, P131 chain to governorate, sitelinks, P856, OSM ids P402/P10689/P11693, P576/P3999/P5817).
5. `fetch_osm_crosscheck.py`: Overpass, **tags only** (no geometry), used to find missed places and to record OSM element ids for items with an explicit `wikidata=*` tag. No OSM coordinates or names go into `places.json`.
6. `build_places.py`: merges everything with `sites_curated.json` (hand-curated site list with QIDs, tiers, categories and egymonuments page mapping) and `museums_manual.json` (exclusions, duplicate merges, additions, overrides). The output is sorted by governorate id, then `name.en`.
7. `validate.py`: stdlib JSON-Schema check against `data/schema/place.schema.json`, plus unique `id`/`wikidataId` and the sort order.
8. `make_report.py`: writes this file.

`wd_search.py` is the helper used while curating QIDs (Wikidata `wbsearchentities` plus a SPARQL describe query).

Data rules: identity, labels and coordinates come only from Wikidata (CC0). When a Wikidata item has no coordinates, `lat`/`lng` are `null`; they are never filled from OSM or from the portal. Names: the EN/AR Wikidata labels are used unless an override in the curation files gives a clearer visitor-facing name (written by us). Governorate: the Wikidata `P131*` chain (also via `P276` / `P361`) to one of the 27 governorates; the defunct Helwan and 6th of October governorates map to Cairo and Giza. When there are several candidates, Luxor and then Cairo win (old Qena and Giza admin links). Status is `unknown` unless Wikidata or our curation records a closure.

### Exact SPARQL

Museum enumeration (union of the two results; a single `P131+ wd:Q79` path query times out on WDQS):

```sparql
SELECT DISTINCT ?item WHERE {
  ?item wdt:P17 wd:Q79 .
  ?item wdt:P31/wdt:P279* wd:Q33506 .
}
```

```sparql
SELECT DISTINCT ?item WHERE {
  ?adm wdt:P17 wd:Q79 .
  ?item wdt:P131 ?adm .
  FILTER NOT EXISTS { ?item wdt:P17 wd:Q79 }
  ?item wdt:P31/wdt:P279* wd:Q33506 .
}
```

Result: 157 museum items (16 excluded, 20 merged as duplicates, 14 added from other sources).

Governorate list (used to build the governorate enum mapping):

```sparql
SELECT ?g ?gLabel ?ar WHERE { ?g wdt:P31 wd:Q204910 . OPTIONAL{?g rdfs:label ?ar FILTER(lang(?ar)="ar")} SERVICE wikibase:label { bd:serviceParam wikibase:language "en". } }
```

Bounding-box sanity check for museums lacking `P17=Egypt` (all 49 hits were in Israel, Palestine, Jordan or Saudi Arabia, so none were added):

```sparql
SELECT DISTINCT ?item ?itemLabel ?coord WHERE {
  SERVICE wikibase:box {
    ?item wdt:P625 ?coord .
    bd:serviceParam wikibase:cornerSouthWest "Point(24.7 22.0)"^^geo:wktLiteral .
    bd:serviceParam wikibase:cornerNorthEast "Point(36.9 31.7)"^^geo:wktLiteral .

}
```

## 3. Notable decisions

- **One record per visitable, ticketed unit.** Tombs and pyramids sold as add-on tickets inside a site (Great Pyramid, Tutankhamun's tomb, Nefertari, Serapeum and others) are not separate records. They are listed in the curation file's `parts` and should become `prices.extras` of the parent site.
- **Museums inside sites are separate records**, for example the Imhotep Museum (Saqqara), Karnak Open-Air Museum, Crocodile Museum (Kom Ombo), Aswan Museum (Elephantine), Tell Basta Museum and San el-Hagar Museum.
- **Galleries inside a museum are not records.** The Tutankhamun galleries and the Khufu Ships Museum are part of GEM, so three Khufu-ships Wikidata items are excluded. The Children's Museum of the Egyptian Museum is part of `egyptian-museum`. The sub-museums of the Agricultural Museum complex in Dokki (Cotton, Plant Resources, Syrian Hall and others) are merged into `agricultural-museum` as aliases.
- **Moved or closed collections:** the Egyptian Museum (Tahrir) stays `unknown`/open, with a note that the Tutankhamun collection moved to GEM. The old Giza Solar Boat Museum and the Citadel Carriage Museum are `closed`, pointing to GEM and to the Bulaq Royal Carriages Museum. The proposed Alexandria Underwater Museum is `closed` (never built).
- **Memphis:** the visitable site is the Mit Rahina Open-Air Museum, so it is seeded once as a museum (`mit-rahina-museum`) and not again as a site.
- **Heritage house-museums** that Wikidata types as museums (Ali Labib House, Beshtak Palace, Gayer-Anderson) are `kind: museum`, even when MoTA sells their tickets on the sites list.
- **Commercial galleries** typed as museums in Wikidata (Espace Karim Francis, Mashrabia) are kept as `confidence: low` and flagged below.
- **Sites total ~135, above the ~100 target.** This follows the MoTA ticket list plus the egymonuments archaeological-sites and monuments pages. Most of the extra are `minor` ticketed Delta, Middle Egypt and Western Desert sites, which an app user may still need. To trim, drop non-ticketed `minor` sites (`mota` = false in `sites_curated.json`).
